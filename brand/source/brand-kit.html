01 · Who we are

Every symptom deserves a real look, not a shrug and a label.

Voiced helps people with Down syndrome, their families, and their clinicians catch what diagnostic overshadowing hides: when a real, treatable symptom gets waved off as “just Down syndrome.”

Mission

Voiced exists so that people with Down syndrome are heard, not overlooked, in healthcare.

Who it's for

Self-advocates with Down syndrome, the families and caregivers beside them, and the clinicians who want to get it right.

What we give them

Tools to prepare for appointments, explain what's normal for them, and speak up, so every visit leads to real answers.

Nothing for me,
without me.

Our guiding principle, borrowed from the disability rights movement. Every tool, page and message is built with self-advocates, not just for them. When in doubt, ask: would the person this is about feel included and respected reading it?

06 · Voice

How Voiced sounds

Like a knowledgeable friend sitting next to you in the waiting room: on your side, plain-spoken, and never talking down.

Plain, not clinical

Short sentences and everyday words. Explain a medical term the first time it appears. If a self-advocate couldn't read it on their own, rewrite it.

Respectful, not pitying

People with Down syndrome are the experts on their own lives. Speak to them directly, not only about them, and never frame them as a burden or a tragedy.

Confident, not alarming

We help people speak up and ask better questions. We never diagnose, and we never promise outcomes. In an emergency, we say call 911.

Say

  • “person with Down syndrome,” “self-advocate”
  • Ask what someone wants to be called, and use it
  • “Bring this summary to your appointment.”
  • “This is new for me, and it isn't how I usually feel.”
  • “It never diagnoses. It helps you advocate.”

Avoid

  • “Down's,” “a Down syndrome child,” “suffers from”
  • Jargon without a plain explanation
  • “Don't worry, it's probably nothing.”
  • Promises like “get the right diagnosis”
  • Cute or childish language for adults
